# Founder decisions 2026-10-30 — copy and wording

Scope: the web `PENDING_FOUNDER_REVIEW` register, the F10–F17 and V2 (A4 outline, D1
export) keys, the G5 corner-credit wording, the never-send lint findings, the two
GLOSSARY §5.2 unclaimed rows, sign-in mail and alert copy, and the EDITORIAL-STANDARD
(L15) Bulgarian labels. Thresholds, the CEMS match rule, the export tier and any data
or licence policy are out of scope: approving the copy that describes them does not
decide them.

The general rule applied: when in doubt, take the wording that claims less (GLOSSARY
§1, §5) and that a Bulgarian reader meets in one consistent form (GLOSSARY §2, review
31). GLOSSARY is frozen; where a decision touches it, it goes in as a dated amendment
rather than an edit to an existing row.
